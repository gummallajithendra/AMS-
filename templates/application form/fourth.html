Certificates to be Enclosed with the Application

The application should be submitted along with the following 3 sets of Xerox copies of certificates. However, the original certificates must be produced at the time of admission.

  1. 10th or SSC / H.S Vocational Certificate / Diploma Certificate
  2. HSC or Intermediate
  3. Transfer Certificate
  4. Conduct Certificate
  5. Community Certificate (e-Certificate)
  6. Extra Curricular Activities Certificate (if any)
  7. General Rank Card issued by Consortium or A.U.
  8. Anna University Allotment Order with Payment Receipt (Single Window Candidates only)
  9. Medical Fitness Certificate with Blood Group
  10. Migration Certificate (Compulsory for students from states other than Tamil Nadu)
  11. Eligibility Certificate (Applicable only for other state students, must be produced at admission)
  12. 5 Recent Passport Size Photos (Name & Department written on back)
  13. If a student claims First Graduate Concession, submit the following certificates from HQ Dy. Tahsildar only in electronic form / digitally signed e-certificate:
    • (i)First Graduate Certificate
    • (ii)Joint Declaration Form
    • (iii)Nativity Certificate
