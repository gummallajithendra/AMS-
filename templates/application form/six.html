JOINT DECLARATION BY THE APPLICANT AND PARENT

We hereby solemnly and sincerely affirm that the statements made and the information furnished by me in the application as also in the enclosures thereto, submitted by me are true.   1. That my son / daughter / ward would strictly adhere to all the rules and regulations in force now or which may be introduced in the institution hereafter and that breach of discipline and rules by my son / daughter / ward entail summary forfeiture of his / her seat in the institution.

  3. That I am aware that the curriculum for the various courses is not rigid and my son / daughter / ward will follow the syllabus for the various courses in force at the time of his / her admission and that any revision or Modification made in the syllabi during the course of his / her study in the Institution will be binding on him / her.

That in case my son / daughter / ward, become a scholarship holder or comes to enjoy educational concessions like half fees or full fees etc and does not show special progress, the scholarships or educational concessions are liable to be cancelled and that if my son’s / daughter’s / ward’s conduct and character are not good these will be cancelled summarily.
  5. That my son / daughter / ward is aware that breach of discipline and rules or bad conduct in the NCC or extracurricular activities will also entail summary forfeiture of seat in the Institution, in addition to such other proceedings that may be taken against him / her.
  6. That I am aware that if my son / daughter / ward is admitted into the hostel he / she will strictly abide by the rules and regulations is force in the hostel and that any breach of discipline or rules or any unruly conduct or undesirable actions will be summarily dealt with be forfeiture of seat both in the hostel and the Institution in addition to such other proceeding that may be taken against him / her.   9. That my son / daughter / ward should not involve himself / herself in any activity prejudice to the proper conduct of study or discipline, or peace in the College Campus such as involving himself / herself in strikes, unlawful assemblies, consumption of intoxicating drinks and drugs and late attendance or any other infringement of rules and regulation and refusing to accept the punishment ordered by the Principal / Management.
  10. I agree to abide by the rules and regulations of the college and I am fully aware that if my son/daughter/ward does not follow the rules and regulations, the college will terminate my sons/daughters/wards studies and to leave the college.
  11. I agree to pay the fees fixed by the Government for self Financing Colleges and Maintenance charges fixed by the Management.
  12. No refund of fees will be entertained, if student discontinue his / her studies for what so ever reason, during the middle of the Semester besides student will be required to pay full fees for the entire academic year.
  13. I agree that I will not participate in the ragging of students. I am aware that it is a serious offence declared by the government punishable under criminal law and also invites issuance of Transfer Certificate.
  14. I will strictly comply with the dress code of the college.
  15. I will not use cell phone in the class rooms and laboratory classes during college hours. I also agree that if there is any revision in fee structure at a later date, the amount payable will be paid.

               We are fully aware of the declaration and I have understood the same. I agree to the above conditions.
